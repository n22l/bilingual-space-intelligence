# Synthetic bilingual fixtures

All documents, publisher labels, and source identifiers here were invented for this demo and are released under the repository MIT license. They assert no real aerospace events, dates, organizations, or economics. `synthetic:` identifiers are not real source URLs. Unknown dates are intentional.

Eight documents yield ten paragraph passages. Six labeled answerable questions test fixture retrieval; two intentionally unsupported questions are for manual inspection only. These authored smoke cases are not a held-out research benchmark.
